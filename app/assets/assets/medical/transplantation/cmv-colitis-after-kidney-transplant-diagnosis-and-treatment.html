CMV Colitis After Kidney Transplant: Diagnosis and Treatment

Why tissue-invasive CMV after prophylaxis needs treatment-dose IV ganciclovir, and how immunosuppression is adjusted

Transplant · Infectious Disease
Clinical context

A 51-year-old woman, 5 months after deceased-donor kidney transplant with ATG induction, on tacrolimus, mycophenolate, and prednisone, presents with fever, diarrhea, and leukopenia. She is CMV donor-positive, recipient-negative (D+/R−), with plasma CMV PCR 185,000 IU/mL. Colonoscopy shows shallow ulcers, and biopsy shows CMV inclusions with positive immunohistochemistry. She completed 3 months of valganciclovir prophylaxis.
