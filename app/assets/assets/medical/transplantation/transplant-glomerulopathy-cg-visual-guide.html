RENAL PATHOLOGY · TRANSPLANT

What is cg?

cg stands for transplant Glomerulopathy — the Banff score for chronic glomerular injury in a kidney allograft. It is defined by duplication of the glomerular basement membrane (GBM), seen as "double contours" or "tram-tracks" on silver stain.

cg = chronic GBM duplication Silver stain (Jones) cg0 → cg3

📊 Banff cg Scoring — Summary

Based on the percentage of glomerular capillary loops showing double contours

Score % of capillary loops with double contours Severity Clinical meaning
cg0 0% None No chronic injury
cg1a Not visible by LM Early (EM only) Duplication only seen on electron microscopy
cg1b ≤25% Mild Early chronic injury
cg2 26–50% Moderate Established chronic injury
cg3 >50% Severe Extensive chronic injury
