Clinical context

A 31-year-old woman develops AKI, thrombocytopenia, and hemolytic anemia 48 hours after an uncomplicated vaginal delivery. She is normotensive, with normal liver enzymes, normal PT/aPTT, and no DIC. ADAMTS13 is 68%, stool Shiga toxin is negative, and C3 is mildly low. Hemolysis and thrombocytopenia worsen despite delivery.
