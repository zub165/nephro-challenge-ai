Check Your Understanding

1. Which light chain is most classically linked to AL amyloidosis?

Lambda light chains are structurally more prone to misfolding into beta-pleated sheet fibrils, so AL amyloidosis is lambda-predominant in roughly 70–80% of cases.

2. A patient has κ/λ ratio of 6.1, low C3/C4, and a proliferative GN on biopsy. Most likely diagnosis?

Proliferative GN + monoclonal immunoglobulin + low complement = PGNMID. The clone is often small and missed on marrow biopsy, and urine protein is predominantly albumin.

3. Which finding favors light chain deposition disease over AL amyloidosis?

LCDD deposits are amorphous and Congo red negative, whereas amyloid is Congo red positive with apple-green birefringence under polarized light.
