📊 Step-by-Step Calculation of spKt/V

Full Daugirdas second-generation calculation with all intermediate values

1

Calculate R (post/pre BUN ratio)

R = Post-BUN / Pre-BUN = 18 / 72 = 0.25
2

Convert treatment duration to hours

t = 240 min ÷ 60 = 4.0 hours
3

Calculate the first term: −ln(R − 0.008t)

0.008 × t = 0.008 × 4.0 = 0.032
R − 0.008t = 0.25 − 0.032 = 0.218
ln(0.218) ≈ −1.523
−ln(0.218) ≈ +1.523
4

Calculate the second term: (4 − 3.5R) × UF/W

4 − 3.5(0.25) = 4 − 0.875 = 3.125
UF/W = 3.0 / 75 = 0.04
3.125 × 0.04 = 0.125
5

Add both terms to get spKt/V

spKt/V = 1.523 + 0.125 = 1.648
spKt/V ≈ 1.65 ✓
