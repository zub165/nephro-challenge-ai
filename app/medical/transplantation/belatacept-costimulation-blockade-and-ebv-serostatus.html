Belatacept: Costimulation Blockade and EBV Serostatus

How belatacept blocks T-cell costimulation, and why EBV serostatus determines eligibility

Transplant · Immunosuppression
Clinical context

Belatacept is a fusion protein used as maintenance immunosuppression in kidney transplant recipients. It is given intravenously and is restricted to EBV-seropositive recipients because of post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder (PTLD) risk.
