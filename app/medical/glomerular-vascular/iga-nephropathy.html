Check Your Understanding

1. A 45-year-old woman with Crohn disease presents with AKI, RBC casts, proteinuria 5 g/g, and normal complements. What is the most likely biopsy finding?

Correct: B. The combination of Crohn disease + RBC casts + nephrotic-range proteinuria + normal complements points to IgA nephropathy with crescents.

2. Where in the glomerulus does IgA deposit in IgA nephropathy?

Correct: C. IgA deposits are found in the mesangium — the axial region between the capillary loops.

3. In the capillary wall cross-section, which layer sits directly beneath the podocyte foot processes?

Correct: C. The order from urinary space → capillary lumen: Podocyte foot processes → Subepithelial space → GBM → Subendothelial space → Endothelium → Capillary lumen.

4. Which disease causes fibrinoid necrosis in the endocapillary region with NO immune deposits on IF?

Correct: C. Pauci-immune crescentic GN (ANCA) shows fibrinoid necrosis with no immune deposits on IF.

5. Where are the "humps" seen on electron microscopy in post-infectious glomerulonephritis?

Correct: A. The classic "humps" of post-infectious GN are dome-like deposits in the subepithelial space.

6. Which structure is inflamed and fibrosed in chronic kidney disease?

Correct: C. The tubulointerstitium is the site of chronic inflammation and fibrosis in all progressive kidney diseases — its degree predicts long-term outcome.
