Privacy Policy

Medical education only. Nephro Challenge AI is for learning and board review. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Do not submit identifiable patient information (PHI).

Nephro Challenge AI ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Nephro Challenge AI platform across web, Android, and Apple iOS applications. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using our services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Name, email address, username
  • Password (stored securely hashed — never in plain text)
  • Profile details you choose to provide (e.g., specialty, bio)
  • Sign-in via Google, Apple, or Microsoft (Outlook) — we receive your provider user ID and email as permitted by that provider

Learning & quiz data

  • Quiz attempts, scores, accuracy, streaks, XP, and leaderboard rankings
  • Questions answered, time spent, weak topics, and saved clinical pearls
  • Daily challenge completion status

Device & technical data

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and browser type (web)
  • IP address and general usage logs for security and performance
  • Authentication tokens stored locally on your device (web: browser storage; mobile: secure storage)

Payment information

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, or our web payment provider. We do not store full credit card numbers. We receive subscription status, plan type, and transaction identifiers from the store.

AI tutor interactions

When you use the AI tutor, your question context (question text and your prompts) may be sent to our AI provider to generate explanations. Do not include patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, or other PHI.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve board-review MCQs, chapter lessons, and animations
  • Track progress, streaks, leaderboards, and personalized weak-topic recommendations
  • Authenticate you across web, Android, and iOS with a single account
  • Process Premium subscriptions and restore purchases
  • Send service-related emails (account, security, subscription) — not marketing spam
  • Monitor abuse, fraud, and technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Hybrid Architecture

Your account and quiz history are stored on our secure backend (PostgreSQL on our server). Some preferences and session tokens are cached locally on your device for faster access and offline-ready UX. Animation files are served from our CDN; only URLs are stored in our database, not the media files themselves.

4. Third-Party Services

We may share limited data with trusted providers who help us operate the app:

  • Google — Sign in with Google (Android & web)
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple (iOS)
  • Microsoft — Sign in with Outlook / Microsoft account (web)
  • OpenAI — AI explanations and question assistance
  • Cloud hosting & CDN — application hosting and animation delivery
  • Apple / Google — in-app purchase and subscription management

These providers have their own privacy policies. We only share what is necessary to deliver the service.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and learning data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal data within 30 days, except where law requires longer retention (e.g., billing records).

6. Security

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, hashed passwords, JWT authentication, and industry-standard server security. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we work continuously to protect your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Nephro Challenge AI is intended for medical students, residents, fellows, and licensed clinicians — generally users aged 17+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact us to request deletion if you believe a child has provided data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or export your personal data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based

9. Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your account:

  • Mobile: Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  • Web: Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account
  • Email: Send a deletion request to privacy@nephrochallenge.ai from your registered email

Deletion is permanent and removes your quiz history, streaks, leaderboard entries, and saved pearls.

10. International Users

Our servers may be located in the United States. By using the app, you consent to transfer and processing of your data in the U.S. and other countries where our service providers operate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version at https://nephrochallenge.ai/privacy.html and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
