🟣 AL Amyloidosis — Why Lambda?

Structural predisposition to misfolding

  • Structural properties: Lambda light chains are more prone to misfolding into beta-pleated sheets
  • Frequency: Lambda causes ~70–80% of AL amyloidosis cases
  • Low κ/λ ratio (e.g., 0.09) is a major clue for lambda-predominant disease
  • Deposition: Fibrils deposit in heart, kidney, nerves, GI, tongue, skin
  • Stain: Congo red positive → apple-green birefringence

🟣 AL Amyloidosis — Clinical Pattern

Nephrotic syndrome + systemic features

  • Kidney: Nephrotic-range proteinuria, bland sediment
  • Heart: Septal wall thickening, preserved EF
  • Nerves: Autonomic dysfunction → orthostatic hypotension
  • Complement: Normal (not immune-complex mediated)
  • SPEP: Normal — free light chain assay detects the clone

Board Pearl — Lambda Predominance + Nephrotic + Heart Thickening = AL Amyloidosis

A very low κ/λ ratio (0.09) points to a lambda-predominant clone. Lambda light chains misfold into amyloid fibrils that deposit in the heart (septal thickening), kidneys (nephrotic proteinuria), and nerves (orthostatic hypotension). Congo red stain is diagnostic.
