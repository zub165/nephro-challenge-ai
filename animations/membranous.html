Check Your Understanding

1. A 53-year-old woman with nephrotic syndrome has a kidney biopsy showing thickened GBM, silver-stain spikes, and subepithelial deposits on EM with granular IgG/C3 on IF. Which autoantigen is most likely associated?

Correct: B. The biopsy is classic for membranous nephropathy. The most common autoantigen is PLA2R (~70–80%), but since it is not listed, the correct answer is THSD7A — the second most common autoantigen in primary membranous nephropathy (2–3%).

2. What is the classic silver stain finding in membranous nephropathy?

Correct: B. In membranous nephropathy, silver stain shows "spikes" — projections of GBM between subepithelial deposits — creating the classic "spike and dome" appearance. Tram-tracking is seen in MPGN; crescents in crescentic GN; mesangial proliferation in IgA nephropathy and lupus.

3. What is the most common autoantigen in primary membranous nephropathy?

Correct: B. PLA2R is the most common autoantigen in primary membranous nephropathy, present in ~70–80% of cases. THSD7A is the second most common (2–3%). The question did not include PLA2R as an option, so THSD7A was the correct answer.

4. Which IF pattern is most characteristic of membranous nephropathy?

Correct: B. Membranous nephropathy shows a granular IF pattern with IgG and C3 along the capillary wall. Linear IgG is seen in anti-GBM disease. Pauci-immune is seen in ANCA vasculitis. "Full house" is seen in lupus nephritis.

5. Which autoantibody is associated with ANCA-associated vasculitis and a pauci-immune crescentic pattern on biopsy?

Correct: C. ANCA-associated vasculitis is characterized by autoantibodies against myeloperoxidase (MPO) or proteinase 3 (PR3). Biopsy shows a pauci-immune crescentic GN — no immune deposits on IF, distinguishing it from membranous nephropathy.

6. A patient with membranous nephropathy has a positive THSD7A antibody. What additional workup should be considered?

Correct: B. Patients with THSD7A-associated membranous nephropathy have an approximately 20% association with malignancy — higher than PLA2R-associated disease. Age-appropriate cancer screening is recommended. PLA2R-associated membranous nephropathy also warrants screening, but the association is stronger for THSD7A.
