Predicting renal outcome in MPO-ANCA vasculitis using light microscopy

NEPHROLOGY · RENAL PATHOLOGY

58-year-old man with rapidly progressive GN and MPO-ANCA

Microscopic hematuria, RBC casts, rapidly progressive kidney dysfunction. MPO-ANCA strongly positive. Biopsy: pauci-immune necrotizing and crescentic GN. Of 30 glomeruli: 8 normal, 16 with cellular crescents, 6 globally sclerotic. No significant immune-complex deposition.

📊 Berden Classification — Summary

Four classes based on light microscopy of ANCA-associated glomerulonephritis

Class Definition This Patient Prognosis (1-yr renal survival) Key Feature
Focal ≥50% normal glomeruli 26.7% (8/30) — NOT met Best (~96%) Pauci-immune necrosis may be present
Crescentic ≥50% cellular crescents 53.3% (16/30) — MET ✅ ~86% Active inflammation — reversible with treatment
Mixed <50% normal AND <50% crescentic AND <50% sclerotic Not met (crescents >50%) Intermediate (~76%) All three lesion types present but none dominant
Sclerotic ≥50% globally sclerotic glomeruli 20.0% (6/30) — NOT met Worst (~57%) Irreversible scar — poor response to immunosuppression
