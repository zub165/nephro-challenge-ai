Key Features: Differential Diagnosis

Disease Site of Injury Urine Findings Lab Clues Management
Tenofovir Tubulopathy Correct Proximal tubule Renal glycosuria, bland sediment, no hematuria Hypokalemia, metabolic acidosis, hypophosphatemia, hypouricemia (Fanconi syndrome) Stop tenofovir, switch to alternative cART
HIVICK Distractor Glomerulus (immune complex) Dysmorphic RBCs, RBC casts, proteinuria Low C3/C4, active sediment Kidney biopsy; consider RAAS blocker, immunosuppression
HIVAN Distractor Glomerulus (collapsing) Heavy proteinuria, bland sediment Normal complement, advanced HIV Optimize cART; consider steroids
Indinavir/Atazanavir Crystals Distractor Tubulointerstitial (crystals) Crystalluria, hematuria, pyuria Nephrolithiasis, AKI Stop offending drug, hydrate

Check Your Understanding

1. Which lab finding is most characteristic of tenofovir-induced proximal tubulopathy?

Correct: B. Tenofovir causes proximal tubular injury with Fanconi syndrome, which includes renal glycosuria (glucose in urine despite normal serum glucose), hypophosphatemia, hypouricemia, hypokalemia, and metabolic acidosis.

2. What is the most appropriate next step in management for tenofovir-induced nephrotoxicity?

Correct: C. The diagnosis is clinically highly likely. The next step is to stop tenofovir and switch to an alternative antiretroviral agent. Kidney function often stabilizes or improves after discontinuation. Biopsy is reserved for unclear cases.

3. Which cART agent is associated with crystal nephropathy and nephrolithiasis?

Correct: B. Indinavir (and to a lesser extent atazanavir) is poorly soluble in urine and can cause crystal deposition, nephrolithiasis, and tubulointerstitial disease.

4. A patient with HIV on cART presents with proteinuria, dysmorphic RBCs, and low complement levels. What is the most likely diagnosis?

Correct: B. HIVICK is an immune complex disease that presents with active urinary sediment (dysmorphic RBCs, RBC casts), proteinuria, and low complement levels. HIVAN typically has normal complement and a bland sediment.
